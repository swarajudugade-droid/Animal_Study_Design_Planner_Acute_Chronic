PHARMACALC • PRECLINICAL RESEARCH PLANNER

Animal Study Design Planner

Structured planning for acute and chronic preclinical studies: scientific question, groups, dosing schedule, observations, endpoints, sample-size rationale, humane endpoints, randomization, and reporting checklist.

Research and ethics notice: This tool helps organize a protocol; it does not authorize animal experiments or replace expert review. Follow the current applicable CPCSEA rules, institutional IAEC approval, veterinarian guidance, and institutional SOPs. Do not begin procedures, dosing, or animal procurement before required approvals. Use the 3Rs—Replacement, Reduction, Refinement—and establish humane endpoints with a qualified veterinarian/IAEC.

1. Study overview

2A. Acute study planning

Use this section for single-exposure or short observation-window studies, acute pharmacology, tolerability, or acute toxicity protocols. Exact procedures and dose levels must come from the approved protocol, relevant guideline, and veterinary/IAEC review.

Acute-design checkpoints
  • Define whether the primary purpose is efficacy, tolerability, or toxicity; these are not interchangeable.
  • Use the applicable current guideline and approved protocol for dose selection and observation requirements.
  • Predefine humane endpoints and emergency veterinary response before study start.
  • Specify how adverse events, unexpected deaths, missing observations, and protocol deviations will be handled.

2B. Chronic / repeated-dose study planning

Use this section for repeated exposure or long-duration efficacy/toxicity studies. The study duration, schedule, and monitoring intensity must be scientifically justified and approved; this planner intentionally does not prescribe dose levels or procedures.

Chronic-design checkpoints
  • Plan longitudinal analysis and account for within-animal correlation and repeated measurements.
  • Define cumulative burden, handling habituation, monitoring responsibility, and veterinary review triggers.
  • Predefine interim review criteria, humane endpoints, recovery assessments, and disposition of animals.
  • Account for cage/litter clustering, sex, batch effects, and possible multiplicity in the analysis plan.

    Suggested workflow

    1. State the hypothesis and primary outcome.
    2. Choose the minimum design that answers the question.
    3. Justify animal numbers statistically or with documented evidence.
    4. Define allocation, blinding, inclusion/exclusion, and analysis before data collection.
    5. Review welfare, humane endpoints, and procedures with qualified staff.
    6. Submit the protocol for applicable institutional and regulatory review.
    7. Report transparently using ARRIVE guidance.

    Design guidance

    Acute studies

    Emphasize a defined exposure and observation window, time-anchored endpoints, observation frequency, safety escalation, and handling of unexpected outcomes. The applicable study-specific guideline determines required details.

    Chronic studies

    Emphasize repeated exposure, longitudinal measures, welfare monitoring over time, recovery/follow-up where justified, cumulative burden, repeated-measures statistics, and preplanned interim decisions.

    ARRIVE 2.0 reporting

    Consider study design, sample-size rationale, inclusion/exclusion criteria, randomization, blinding, outcome measures, statistical methods, animal details, procedures, results, and protocol registration where applicable.

    India-specific oversight

    Confirm current CPCSEA rules, institutional IAEC procedures, facility permissions, and the relevant official guideline for the planned study type. This app does not verify the current regulatory version.

    Reference points for protocol development

    • ARRIVE Guidelines 2.0: reporting guidance for animal research; use the current official checklist.
    • NC3Rs Experimental Design Assistant: supports structured experimental design and bias-control planning.
    • Applicable CPCSEA / IAEC requirements: use current official documents and institution-specific SOPs for India.

    This planner does not calculate doses, prescribe animal procedures, or determine regulatory compliance. Review all study-specific details with qualified experts.
